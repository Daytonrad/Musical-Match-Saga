Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy describes how the Musical Match Saga web game (“this game”) handles information when you play it in your browser.

In short: the game has no accounts and no analytics, advertising or tracking code. Your progress is saved in your browser's localStorage for this site and is not sent anywhere by the game.

What is stored in your browser

The game saves data under a single localStorage key (musicalMatchSaga) for this site. It contains:

  • your high score and the highest level you have reached;
  • if you leave a level part-way through, a saved copy of that level so you can continue: the level number, lives, score, total score, moves left, timer, the board layout, the objective counters and a timestamp.

This data is only used to restore your game when you come back. It contains no name, email address or other account details. The game's code does not send it to any server.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask you to sign in or create an account.
  • It does not contain analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not show ads.
  • Its own code makes no network requests to send data about you or your play anywhere. The game's code, styles and font are all served from this site.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests the pages. I do not receive or control that data, and I have not checked exactly what GitHub records; see GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Your choices

  • You can remove your saved progress at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser's settings. Your high score, highest level and any saved level will be lost.
  • Data in localStorage stays on the device and browser you used, and is not shared between devices.

Changes

If the game changes in a way that affects what it stores or sends, this page will be updated and the date above changed.

Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
